About

Sesame never says “Closed” unless the camera positively saw the tag on the door. An open door, a dark frame, a sleeping PC, or a camera that won't answer all come out the same way: couldn't confirm. It is built to nag rather than reassure you wrongly.

This app has no way to open or close the door — by design.
